WHY RESPONSIBLE AI?

AI is transforming education. But is it responsible?

Generative AI can support learning, but it can also produce harmful content, reinforce stereotypes, generate misleading information and encourage academic dishonesty.

OUR CAPABILITIES

What does our platform do?

Evaluate educational content through four essential dimensions of responsible AI.

🛡️

Safety & Toxicity

Identifies potentially harmful, abusive, toxic or inappropriate content.

⚖️

Fairness & Bias

Evaluates stereotypes, discriminatory assumptions and unequal representation.

📚

Academic Integrity

Flags content that may encourage cheating, plagiarism or unsupported claims.

🎓

Pedagogical Relevance

Evaluates clarity, educational suitability, reading level and learning relevance.

OUR APPROACH

Building trust in educational AI.

Our framework combines AI-powered content evaluation with structured risk reporting and historical audit records. It is designed to support educators and institutions in making more informed decisions.
